Essential Rules

Water Safety Rules

Five golden rules every child should know before entering any body of water.

01

Never Swim Alone

Always swim with a buddy or adult supervisor. The buddy system saves lives.

02

Walk, Don't Run

Pool decks are slippery. Walking prevents falls and dangerous accidents.

03

Look Before You Leap

Never dive into unknown water. Check depth and look for obstacles first.

04

Use Safety Gear

Wear a life jacket in open water. Floaties are toys, not life-saving devices.

05

Call for Help

If someone is in trouble, shout for help. Never jump in to rescue alone.

★

Learn CPR Basics

Every parent should know basic CPR. We offer free workshops monthly.

Why This Matters

Drowning is the leading cause of unintentional injury death for children ages 1-4. For every child who dies from drowning, another five receive emergency care for nonfatal submersion injuries. Formal swimming lessons can reduce the risk of drowning by 88% among children aged 1-4.

Our Commitment

We are committed to making water safety education accessible to every child. Through scholarships, community programs, and free workshops, we ensure that financial barriers never prevent a child from learning to be safe in water.

Learning Path

Your Child's Safety Journey

We follow a structured progression that takes children from complete water beginners to confident, safety-conscious swimmers. Each stage builds on the previous one.

Step 01

Water Comfort

Get comfortable with water on the face, blowing bubbles, and basic floating with support.

Step 02

Basic Skills

Learn kicking, arm movements, and unassisted floating. Introduction to freestyle.

Step 03

Safety Awareness

Understand pool rules, recognize dangerous situations, and learn basic rescue techniques.

Step 04

Confident Swimmer

Master all four strokes, build endurance, and develop water survival skills for life.

Common Questions

Water Safety FAQs

At what age should my child start swimming lessons?
We recommend starting lessons as early as 6 months for parent-and-child classes. For independent lessons, age 3 is ideal. However, it's never too late — we have programs for all ages up to 14.
How many lessons until my child can swim?
Every child progresses at their own pace. Typically, 20-30 lessons are needed for basic swimming competency. Our structured curriculum ensures steady progress with regular assessments.
What if my child is afraid of water?
Fear of water is completely normal. Our instructors are specially trained in gentle introduction techniques. We never rush a child — comfort comes first, skills follow naturally.
Do you offer private lessons?
Yes! We offer one-on-one private lessons for children who need extra attention or want to accelerate their learning. Private lessons can be scheduled at your convenience.
