Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

Everything you need to know about starting swimming lessons with confidence.

What if my child is afraid of water or cries during their first class?

Hesitation is completely normal! Our instructors are specifically trained in gentle acclimation. We never force submersion. We start on our shallow teaching ledge with gentle song games, floating toys, and bubble blowing until your child feels comfortable and secure.

What is the water temperature and why does it matter?

Our pool is heated to a tropical 90°F (32°C). Cold water causes children's muscles to tense, teeth to chatter, and anxiety to rise. In our 90°F warm water, children's muscles relax, allowing them to focus happily on breath control and stroke learning.

How strict is the 4:1 student-to-instructor ratio?

Strict and guaranteed! We cap all group classes at a maximum of 4 children per certified instructor. This ensures your child spends their entire session actively swimming and receiving constant feedback, rather than waiting on the pool steps.

What should we bring for the first lesson?

Just a fitted swimsuit, a dry towel, and a smile! For children under 3 years old, a reusable swim diaper is required. We provide all sanitized kickboards, goggles, fins, and Coast Guard-approved flotation gear at no extra charge.

How do progress ribbons and level advancement work?

Our curriculum has 4 progressive levels. Coaches continuously record skills in our system. Whenever your child masters a key skill (e.g. 10-second back float or rhythmic freestyle kick), they receive a waterproof ribbon at the end of class, and advance to the next level when ready!
