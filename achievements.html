Age-Based Learning

Skill Milestones

Every child develops at their own pace. Our age-based milestone system provides clear expectations and structured progression for parents and students alike.

3
Ages 3–4

Water Comfort & Safety

Enter and exit water safely, blow bubbles, float with assistance, and understand basic pool rules.

5
Ages 5–6

Independent Floating & Kicking

Float unassisted for 20 seconds, kick across the pool with a board, and jump in with recovery.

7
Ages 7–9

Stroke Introduction & Endurance

Swim freestyle and backstroke for 15 meters, tread water for 1 minute, and learn rescue basics.

10
Ages 10–14

Advanced Strokes & Lifesaving

Master all four competitive strokes, swim 100 meters continuously, and complete rescue certification.
