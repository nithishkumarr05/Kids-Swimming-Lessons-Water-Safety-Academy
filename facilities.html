Safety First

Safety Equipment

Comprehensive safety equipment available poolside and throughout our facility.

Life Rings & Buoys

Multiple rescue rings and torpedo buoys mounted at every corner of the pool for immediate access.

Reaching Poles

Long rescue poles placed strategically around the deck to assist anyone in distress without entering the water.

First Aid Stations

Fully stocked first aid kits and AED devices located in the pool area and changing rooms.

Pool Lifts

ADA-compliant pool lifts for swimmers with mobility challenges, ensuring everyone can enjoy the water.

Life Jackets

Various sizes of US Coast Guard-approved life jackets available free for all students and parents.

Emergency Phones

Dedicated emergency phone lines near the pool deck connecting directly to local emergency services.

Spacious Changing Rooms

Deep Cleaning 3x Daily

Our pool deck, changing rooms, and common areas are deep-cleaned three times each day using child-safe, eco-friendly disinfectants.

Water Quality Testing

Pool water is tested hourly for pH, chlorine, and bacteria levels. Results are posted daily for transparency.

HEPA Air Filtration

Our HVAC system uses hospital-grade HEPA filters with UV-C light, circulating fresh air every 6 minutes.

Hand Sanitizer Stations

Touchless hand sanitizer stations are placed throughout the facility, including at every entrance and exit.

Sanitized Flooring

All flooring is non-slip and sanitized regularly with child-safe cleaning solutions that don't irritate skin.

Trained Cleaning Staff

Our dedicated cleaning team is trained in child-safe sanitation practices and works around the clock.
